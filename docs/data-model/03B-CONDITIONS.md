# Purpose

This document defines the canonical Condition entity for Freshwater Fishing Companion.

Conditions describe the environmental, seasonal, and situational factors that influence fishing decisions.

Conditions are shared by Fish, Techniques, Rigs, Learning Articles, Locations, and the recommendation engine.

Each condition exists once and is referenced throughout the Companion.

---

# Design Philosophy

Conditions answer the question:

**"What is happening right now?"**

Recommendations are generated by combining conditions with fish, techniques, rigs, and the user's available tackle.

The Companion shall use canonical Condition records rather than duplicate descriptive text across multiple entities.

---

# Canonical Entity

Every Condition inherits the Foundation entity.

Required base fields:

```text
id
name
summary
createdVersion
lastModifiedVersion
isActive
```

Additional Condition fields extend the base entity.

# Condition Fields

## category

Purpose

Groups similar conditions.

Ownership

Application.

Examples

- Water
- Weather
- Season
- Time
- Habitat
- Structure
- Waterbody
- Fishing Pressure
- Experience

---

## parentConditionId

Purpose

Allows hierarchical organization.

Ownership

Application.

Example

```text
Water Clarity
    ├── Clear
    ├── Slightly Stained
    ├── Stained
    └── Muddy
```

---

## recommendationWeight

Purpose

Relative influence on recommendation calculations.

Ownership

Application.

Rules

Weights are internal values used by the recommendation engine and are not shown to users.

---

## relatedConditionIds

Purpose

References commonly associated conditions.

Ownership

Application.

Example

```text
Spring Spawn

↓

Shallow Water
```

---

## imageIds

Purpose

Illustrations for learning content.

Ownership

Application.

# Recommendation Engine

Conditions may influence:

- Fish likelihood
- Technique recommendations
- Rig recommendations
- Learning suggestions

The recommendation engine shall evaluate multiple conditions together rather than relying on a single condition.

---

# Design Notes

Conditions describe circumstances.

They do not describe:

- Fish
- Rigs
- Techniques
- Inventory

Those entities reference Conditions rather than duplicating them.

---

# Future Enhancements

Potential future additions include:

- Automatic seasonal calculations
